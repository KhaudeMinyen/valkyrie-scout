Build Notes
  • ELRS radio not included — assumes you already own a RadioMaster TX16S or similar ELRS 2.4 GHz transmitter. Add ~$80 if buying new.
  • Charger optional — you may already own one. Remove $55 from total if so.
  • Camera optional — RunCam Thumb is listed for SAR utility. Remove $55 for a basic trainer build.
  • FC price — Matek H743-SLIM V3 is ~$52 on rcdrone.top; listed as sold-out on Pyrodrone at $129, so shop around or use rcdrone.top.
  • Wing spars — full-span design: 10 mm main spar at 25–30% chord handles bending, 6 mm anti-rotation rod at 65–70% chord prevents the wing from pivoting. Both run root-to-tip (700 mm) and seat about 70 mm into the printed wing holder on the fuselage roof.
  • Hot-wire bow — can be built free from scrap wood + nichrome wire. Remove from BOM if you have materials.
  • PETG-CF spool cost — pro-rated from $30/kg; structural nodes + motor mount print in ~250 g.
  • ArduPlane free — open-source firmware, $0. Mission Planner GCS also free.
  • Eagle Scout pitch — this BOM is fundable via Rotary Club (~$500 grants), AMA Foundation or Home Depot Foundation. Donor: Central Florida county emergency management or search-and-rescue team.
