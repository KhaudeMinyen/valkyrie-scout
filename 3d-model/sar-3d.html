Check before you cut

  • Boom spacing 325 clears the prop The blueprint's 234 mm put the booms inside the 254 mm prop circle. At 325 each boom sits about 30 mm outside the blade tips.
  • Tail volume now 0.38, up from 0.28 Stable beginner planes sit around 0.35–0.5. The 160 mm tail chord gets there, and the booms grew to 640 mm so the wider tail still sits behind the prop. The elevator is one 309 × 64 mm panel between the fins, driven by a servo at each end; the 95 mm stubs outboard of each fin stay fixed.
  • Prop now sits 23 mm above the belly line The motor is raised to 150 mm on a printed pylon above the fuselage roof, so a belly landing hits foam first. A high thrust line pitches the nose down a little when you add throttle; ArduPlane trim or a degree or two of motor downthrust takes it out. The prop plane sits only 22 mm ahead of the tail, so keep that gap.
  • CG target: 70–84 mm behind the leading edge That is 25–30% of chord. Slide the battery fore and aft to hit it before the first flight.
